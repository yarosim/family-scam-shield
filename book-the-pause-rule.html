The Pause Rule

How to Talk With Your Parents About Scams and Build a Family Safety Plan Before Anything Goes Wrong

by Hollis M. Wren · PNS Global LLC

A calm, practical guide for adult children and their parents to build a shared Family Safety Plan before a scam call arrives. It covers the pressure tactics behind expensive scams, how to start the conversation, six simple household rules, practice drills, and what to do in the first 24 hours if money has already been sent.
