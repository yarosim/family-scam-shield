About Simon Simon MSc

Simon Simon, MSc, writes plain-English guides for families and small businesses, published by PNS Global LLC. His books cover home and small-business cybersecurity, everyday uses of AI, small-business automation, beginner personal finance, habit change, and a short daily devotional.

Other PNS Global LLC lines are written under the pen names Hollis M. Wren (The Family Scam Shield) and Callum R. Varga (From Chart to Plan).

The field of the MSc is not listed here because it is not recorded in the publisher files. Contact: pnsgloballlc@gmail.com.
